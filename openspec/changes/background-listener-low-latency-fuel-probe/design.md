# Design: Low-Latency Wi-Fi OBD, Always-Connected Background Listener, and 7C0 Fuel Probe

## Decisions
1. **Wi-Fi Stall Mitigation**: Acquire `WIFI_MODE_FULL_LOW_LATENCY` + `PARTIAL_WAKE_LOCK` inside `WifiNetworkBinder` and switch `LocationTracker` to hardware `LocationManager.GPS_PROVIDER` when `connectionType == WIFI_TCP` to prevent 802.11 PSM (`102.4 ms` DTIM sleep) and Play Services FLP off-channel Wi-Fi scans. Combine with bulk TCP `ByteArray(256)` reads, `IPTOS_LOWDELAY`, `650 ms` live CAN timeout, `ATAT1` (avoiding `ATAT2` premature drops), and session-sticky multi-PID disable when an ECU never responds to multi-PID.
2. **Process-Scoped Background Listener**: `ApexObdApplication` implements `ViewModelStoreOwner` so `HudViewModel` and `ObdPollingEngine` stay alive when `MainActivity` moves to the background or exits via Back. `LoggingForegroundService` uses `ServiceCompat.startForeground` with `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_TYPE_CONNECTED_DEVICE` (plus `LOCATION` when granted) to prevent Android 14/15 `SecurityException` on launch. The notification's `contentIntent` dispatches `ACTION_TERMINATE`, triggering clean trip flush, stats persist, adapter disconnect, lock release, and `stopSelf()`. Re-opening the app (`MainActivity.onStart()`) resumes the connection automatically.
3. **Combination Meter (`7C0`) Fuel-Level Probing**: Keep `EcuDiscoveryDumper` strictly read-only (Modes `01`/`02`/`03`/`06`/`07`/`09`/`0A`/`21`/`22`), add full `7E0` Mode 21 bitmask walk plus `7C0`/`750`/`7E1`/`7E2` header probes with `finally` restore to `ATCRA` + `ATSH7DF`, and wire `headerHex` header switching into `ObdPollingEngine` for custom PIDs.
